I am a Ph.D. student at The University of Texas at Austin, advised by [Prof. Shuchi Chawla](https://www.cs.utexas.edu/~shuchi/).

My research interests lie at the intersection of computer science and economics, including mechanism design, online algorithms, and voting theory.

Previously, I was an undergraduate in the [Yao Class at Tsinghua University](https://iiis.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/Yao_Class/About_Yao_Class.htm). In spring and summer 2022, I visited the University of Michigan and enjoyed working with [Prof. Thatchaphol Saranurak](https://sites.google.com/site/thsaranurak/) and [Prof. Seth Pettie](https://web.eecs.umich.edu/~pettie/).

Outside research, I enjoy sports, especially soccer and basketball.
